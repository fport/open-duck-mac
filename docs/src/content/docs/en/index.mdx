This site is the full version of the experiments behind our short videos. Every script, log and measurement is in the repository, so you don't have to take our word for anything: run it again on your own Mac.

## Results in brief

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="72.4 M steps in 3.57 h" icon="rocket">
    Apple M5 MacBook, 6 cores, 1,536 parallel ducks. The Mac's GPU cannot run this physics, so everything runs on the CPU.
  </Card>
  <Card title="First step at 68.4 M" icon="star">
    Stood up in ~11 minutes, sat on a plateau for ~3 hours, then lifted its feet: 29.8 cm in 5 s.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Break the reward, get a hack" icon="warning">
    "Be fast" → crawling on its belly at 14.9 min. "Don't waste energy" → frozen at 7.5 min. "Lift your feet" → lying down, feet in the air, at 30 min.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Half the distance on a real motor" icon="setting">
    The simulated motor limit is ~70 % above the catalog rating. At the catalog limit the same policy covers half the distance.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

:::caution[Honest limits]
Everything is simulation; there is no real robot. Each experiment is one seed, one run. The official recipe is 150 M steps on an NVIDIA GPU; we stopped at 48 %. Details at the end of each part.
:::
